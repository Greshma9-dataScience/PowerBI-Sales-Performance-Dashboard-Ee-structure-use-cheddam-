Business Insights — Sales Performance Analysis

1. Project Summary

This project analyzes six sample sales orders to understand product-wise revenue and sales performance.

2. Key Metrics

Metric| Result
Total Revenue| ₹1,56,000
Total Orders| 6
Total Quantity Sold| 16 units
Average Revenue per Order| ₹26,000

3. Product-wise Revenue

Product| Revenue
Laptop| ₹90,000
Mobile| ₹50,000
Headphones| ₹16,000
Total| ₹1,56,000

4. Business Insights

Insight 1: Laptop is the top revenue contributor

Laptop sales generated ₹90,000, the highest revenue among the three products.

Insight 2: Mobile is the second-highest contributor

Mobile sales generated ₹50,000 in revenue.

Insight 3: Headphones generated the lowest revenue

Headphones generated ₹16,000. Further analysis of units sold, pricing, and costs would help explain their performance.

Insight 4: Average revenue per order

The average revenue per order is ₹26,000, calculated as total revenue divided by six orders.

5. Recommendations

- Monitor laptop sales because laptops generate the highest revenue in this sample.
- Analyze product costs and profit margins before making pricing decisions.
- Collect more sales records to identify reliable trends.
- Compare sales across dates and regions when more data becomes available.
